What to expect before you book.

Four chairs, each with its own mirror, in a room of dark wood and warm light with a radio kept low enough to talk over. These are the practical details that decide most bookings.

Appointments
Every service is booked ahead for a set time with one barber. Choose the service first, because the length of the slot depends on it.
Walk-ins
Seated only when a chair is free between booked clients. The booking page shows which chairs are open.
Your first visit
The consultation is part of your slot, not an extra. Describe what you want, or bring a photo, before the clippers come out.
Running late
Arrive at your start time. After ten minutes the barber may shorten the service or move you to the next opening.
Cancelling
Cancel or reschedule at least twelve hours ahead so the slot can go to someone else.
Payment
Card or cash. Tips can be added by card at the chair.
